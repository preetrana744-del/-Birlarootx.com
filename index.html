How does ordering work?+

Sign in and submit an order request. The team confirms stock, the invoice seller, serviceability, delivery charges and the final total before acceptance. Online payment is not currently connected.

When will my order arrive?+

Estimated dispatch is 2–3 working days after order confirmation, followed by an estimated 3–7 working days in transit. Serviceability, charges and the order-specific estimate are confirmed before acceptance. Shipping & Delivery Policy.

What if my item is wrong, damaged or has a broken seal?+

Contact support promptly, preferably within 7 calendar days of delivery, with your order reference and available photos. An unboxing video is not mandatory. Defect-related statutory rights remain available. Returns, replacements & refunds.

Can I return a sealed or opened pack if I change my mind?+

Change-of-mind returns are not offered for correctly supplied supplements, whether sealed or opened. This does not prevent valid claims for defective, unsafe, wrong or misdescribed goods. Read the eligibility and refund process.

Can I cancel my order?+

Contact support with your order reference before dispatch. The team confirms cancellation after checking the status. After dispatch, contact support for interception or return options. Cancellation Policy.

How can I contact support?+

Email rootx1212@gmail.com or call +91 84488 75888. Support hours: Monday–Saturday, 10 am–6 pm IST, excluding public holidays. Acknowledgement target: within 48 hours. Support and escalation.
